- Utility Main Menu Option
	I need to be able to dump all trades executed to a CSV file. Probably in descending order by date.

- Reduce Mapping Pain
	Right now I am separating business (domain) models from data (entity) models for decoupling and maintainability. 
	However, even with an automated mapping library, mapping can be tedious.
	Something I could consider is using projection at query time. This could look like reading the entire entity but explicitly selecting the properties I want to use in the business model.
	Or maybe I simply abandon Entity Framework and Entities and use a more lightweight approach like Dapper or even raw SQL queries.
