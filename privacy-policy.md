Privacy Policy
==============

In Short
--------
Capta Trader doesn't log or share your personal information.

**We don't track you. We don't profile you.**

Data you enter or that is collected towards the functionality of the app is saved locally within the application folder, on your device only. API keys are saved in your operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager, or the Secret Service on Linux), never in the app folder.

The app talks only to the services you choose: your selected market data source (for example, Coinbase Exchange's public price API) and your selected brokerage. Those services receive what any client of theirs does, such as your IP address and, for brokerages, your API keys and orders.
